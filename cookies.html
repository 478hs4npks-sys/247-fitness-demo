Cookie Policy

Last updated October 18, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how cookies and similar technologies are used on the 24-7 Fitness Monticello website.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website or a third-party service stores on your device. They can keep a site working, remember settings or, for some services, track activity across sites.

What we use

This site does not set its own advertising or analytics cookies. The only cookies or similar data you may encounter come from services built into the page:

SourceWhat it doesType
Netlify (hosting)Delivers the site and processes contact form submissions. It may use technical identifiers needed to operate and protect the service.Essential
Google FontsLoads the typefaces used on the site. Google receives your IP address and browser information when fonts load.Functional
Google Maps embedShows the map in the Hours & location section. Google may set cookies or read existing ones when the map loads.Functional / third-party

Third-party cookies

We do not control cookies set by Google or other third parties. Their use of information is covered by their own policies. If you tap a link to Apple Maps, Google Maps or Facebook, you leave our site and their cookie practices apply.

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser or device settings. Blocking some cookies may stop the embedded map from working correctly. The rest of the site will keep working.

  • Safari on iPhone: Settings, Safari, Block All Cookies
  • Chrome on Android: Chrome menu, Settings, Privacy and security, Third-party cookies
  • Most browsers also offer a private browsing mode that clears cookies when you close it

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

We do not run ad tracking or sell personal data, so there is nothing to opt out of on this site.

Changes

If we add analytics, advertising or other tools that use cookies, we will update this page and add a way to manage your choices.
