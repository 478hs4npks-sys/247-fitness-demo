Privacy Policy

Last updated October 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information 24-7 Fitness Monticello ("we," "us") collects through this website and how we use it. We keep it simple because the site is simple.

What we collect

Information you give us. If you use the contact form, we receive your name, your phone number or email, and your message.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, our hosting provider may log technical data such as IP address, browser type, device type, pages requested and the date and time of each request. We use it to keep the site secure and working.

We do not run advertising trackers or analytics tools on this site. We do not sell your personal information.

How we use it

  • To reply to your questions about memberships, hours, supplements and the gym
  • To operate, secure and improve the website
  • To comply with legal obligations

We use your contact details only to respond to you. We do not add you to a marketing list unless you ask us to.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us run the site, and only as needed:

ServicePurpose
NetlifyWebsite hosting and contact form delivery
Google (Fonts and Maps)Typefaces and the embedded map. Google may receive your IP address and browser details when these load.
Apple Maps / Google MapsDirections, if you tap a maps link. You leave our site and their policies apply.
FacebookIf you follow our link to our Facebook page, Facebook's policies apply.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights, safety or property.

How long we keep it

We keep contact form messages only as long as needed to respond and for a reasonable period afterward, then delete them. Server logs are kept by our host according to its own retention schedule.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Residents of some states, including Minnesota, have additional rights under state privacy laws, such as the right to access, correct and delete personal data. To make a request, call (763) 295-4194 or stop by the gym. We will respond within the time required by law.

Cookies

See our Cookie Policy for details.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
